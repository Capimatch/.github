# Capimatch

**Opportunities that find you.**

</div>

---

## The problem

Opportunities are everywhere: scholarships, workshops, hackathons, courses, internships and jobs.
But they are scattered across university pages, social media, posters and group chats,
so they keep reaching the same few people. Everyone else finds out too late, or never.

It is not a lack of talent or motivation. It is a lack of information:
**information does not travel to people. People have to go looking for it.**

## What Capimatch does

We bring opportunities together in one place
and match them to each person's profile, so you stop searching for opportunities
and the right ones find you.

## How it works

1. **Tell us about yourself in two minutes:** what you study, what you know and what you want to learn.
2. **We gather the opportunities:** from universities, clubs, organizations and companies, all in one place.
3. **We match them to you:** you get a daily set of recommendations, each with the reason it fits you.
4. **Capi keeps you posted:** our capybara guide sends you a weekly email with three opportunities picked for you.

## Where we are going

- **Find opportunities automatically:** track new opportunities from universities, government, NGOs, incubators and companies.
- **Make learning a habit:** earn coins, keep streaks and unlock real rewards every time you show up.
- **Reach everyone:** lightweight notifications that work with poor signal or limited data.

## What we believe

- **Free for talent, always.** Finding and signing up for opportunities will never cost you anything.
- **Every opportunity deserves to be seen.** Organizers can share theirs with us so they reach the right people.
- **Talent is everywhere. Information should be too.**

## Status

We are building our first version for university students in Puebla, Mexico.
Want to share an opportunity or collaborate? Reach out at `hello@capimatch.com`.
